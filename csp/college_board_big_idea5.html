Big Idea 5 IMPACT OF COMPUTING

Video 5.1-5.2

  • Computing Innovation -- > A new creation that uses computing to solve daily life problems or improve the quality of life
  • Automation -- > The use of computing to perform tasks without human input being required
  • Data Privacy -- > Protecting personal data from unauthorized access
  • Digital Footprint -- > The impact and record of everything done by a person online

Video 5.3-5.4

  • Bias in Computing -- > Unfair or misleading outcomes caused by biased data or algorithms that might occur because of human nature
  • Ethical Implications -- > Moral concerns raised by biased computing outcomes that might be harmfull
  • Crowdsourcing -- > Getting input from a large group of people via the internet
  • Citizen Science -- > Public participation in scientific research through computing tools
  • Open Data -- > Publicly available data

Video 5.5- 5.6

  • Intellectual Property -- > Legal rights over creations like software, music, and digital art.
  • Plagiarism -- > Copying or using someone else's work without permission or giving any credit
  • Encryption -- > Converting data into a secure format to prevent unauthorized access and decrease the chance of data leakage
  • Phishing -- > Trying to convince internet users to share personal information by undercovering dangerous software by making it look like a trustfull source

Video 3.13-3.14

  • Logic -- > Correctness and reasoning in a program. Basically code can be diveded into 2 parts: Logic and Syntax. Logic is the purpose and syntax is the grammer of the code.
  • Libraries -- > Prewritten code modules for common tasks. These libaries conssit useful datasets and function for the coders. For an example SwiftUI is very important to deasign a user interface.
