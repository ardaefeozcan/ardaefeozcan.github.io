Big Idea 4 COMPUTING SYSTEMS AND NETWORKS

Video 4.1-4.3-4.4

  • Software -- > Programs and operating systems that cont on hardware and control its operations
  • IP Address -- > A unique id like identifier assigned to each device on a network to identify them
  • Network -- > A group of connected devices that can share resources and data
  • Redundancy -- > Having multiple pathways in a network to be sure reliability and fault tolerance of network
  • Bandwidth -- > The maximum amount of data that can be transmitted
  • Latency -- > The time delay between sending and receiving data
  • Packet -- > A small chunk of data sent over a network, which can be reassembled at the destination
